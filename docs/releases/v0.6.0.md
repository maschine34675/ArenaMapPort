# ArenaMapPort 0.6.0

14 November 2026. Chop Shop, the sixth Arena map, joins Equator, Bay5, Air Pit, Bowl and Iceberg. Every map has more Bot spawn rows
and its exits stand in the team start areas, and the sun no longer reaches Chop Shop's far walls through the roof.

## Changes since 0.5.0

- **Chop Shop (new map).** The garage hall with its walled street yard from Escape from Tarkov: Arena (Arena's `Arena_AutoService`) as
  a sixth location, "Arena: Chop Shop". It uses Arena's TeamFight layout: a team pocket at the east end and one at the west end, one
  capture point and four moving gates (the big gate, the equator shutter and two shutters), on a play area of about 80 x 47 m. The map
  has Arena's own scenes, materials and colliders, a newly baked NavMesh for bots, Arena's room acoustics (7 rooms) and ambient
  sounds, and two exits (an east and a west gate). Time of day and weather follow the server, as on Bay5. No loot and no scav raids,
  as on every map. What Chop Shop has:
  - Arena's own spectator crowd: 30 fans in the stands, with the calls, the murmur and the reactions of the other crowd maps (four
    groups of spectators for the murmur and the roar beds).
  - Original per-light flares for its 8 gas lamps.
  - Arena's gate and door sounds for its four gates (four clips, played when a mode plugin releases held gates), Arena's door and gate
    end positions (23 entries, among them four lamp switches) and four TV screens, all with a face, for the ScoreMonitors contract
    (ArenaMode's score display). Like the other maps, these do nothing in an ordinary raid; a mode plugin claims the screens through the
    ScoreMonitors contract (unchanged). Whether ArenaMode offers Chop Shop or uses them there is ArenaMode's decision; this project has
    run no ArenaMode match on this map.
  - Loading screens, a menu banner, a selection marker on EFT's region map (moved twice at the author's request so that it does not cover
    another region's label; checked on the test host's selection screen) and an official description in Arena's own words.
  - What it does not have: **no DynamicMaps overview**, **no native water** and **no winter or snow look** (summer set only; the three
    winter-only ambient loops are not ported).
  - Provisional: the ambient sound mix (3 dB, offset 0.2, range 3) is a computed start value. The author listened to the whole map on
    14 November 2026 and asked for no change (see "Verification and known limitations"). The player rows (an east and a west pair) are a first
    guess, like Bowl's.
- **More Bot spawn rows on all six maps.** 39 Bot rows were appended: Chop Shop 16 (was 7), Equator 16 (10), Bay5 16 (10), Iceberg 17
  (9), Air Pit 20 (14) and Bowl 21 (17); the Player rows stay at 4 per map. Existing rows are unchanged byte for byte (ArenaMode compiles
  six of them in by id and exact coordinates). For a mode plugin the rows widen the pool that bots and pickups are placed from; ArenaMode's
  Team Deathmatch and Domination use their own points. The server data of all six maps changed, so the new server DLL and a server
  restart are part of the update.
- **Exits stand in the team start areas, with their green flares.** On every map the two exits moved into the team start areas (Equator's
  already stood in its booths; Air Pit's south gate stands at the mouth of its start area). The flares now mark the start areas. In a plain
  raid you therefore spawn a few metres from an 8-second exit; Air Pit's north gate cannot be reached in a plain raid until a mode
  plugin releases the passport gates; bots never use exits; in the two ArenaMode Team Deathmatch runs on Bowl and Iceberg ArenaMode
  hid the extraction and nobody extracted. The exits keep their names, so a few no longer describe the place. The DynamicMaps overview markers of Bay5, Air Pit, Bowl and Iceberg
  moved with them.
- **Sun on far walls (fix).** Unity's sun shadows end at the shadow distance, and beyond it EFT keeps the sun out only with simplified shadow stand-ins that
  belong to a level-of-detail group. Chop Shop's hall is wider than that distance, and the far walls showed a sunlit patch with a curved
  edge that followed the view ("the round shadow"); its two shadow hulls are now enrolled and the patch is gone. Iceberg's cafe had the same
  kind of leak at shadow quality Low (low morning sun through the cafe's east glazing lit the lobby behind it; Ultra showed none, Medium
  and High were not checked); its cafe's three shells are enrolled too. **0.5.0 has that Iceberg leak at shadow quality Low.** New developer settings
  `Lighting (arena_autoservice)/RoofDistantShadows` and `Lighting (arena_iceberg)/RoofDistantShadows` (default on, need
  `General/DeveloperMode`); Equator's own roof-shadow key is unchanged.
- **Air Pit:** a floating board above two crates was left out of the map (package v10); the crates are still there. Two of Air Pit's
  three bundles are therefore different from 0.5.0's.
- **The roar beds are 6 dB louder.** The six cheer and angry recordings are exported 6 dB louder than in 0.5.0 (-26 instead of -32 dBFS
  RMS) and the developer key `Spectators/ReactionBedVolume` is back at 1.0. A saved 2.0 moves once to 1.0 (a hidden revision key); any
  other value stays. **Nobody has listened to this level yet.**
- **Iceberg's lamp limits are 4.6 and 1.8** (were 4.8 and 1.9; the author's change of 7 November, chosen by eye). A saved 4.8 / 1.9 pair
  moves once; a tuned pair stays. An Iceberg raid of the release build on the test host ran with 4.6 / 1.8 (5 materials capped, 15
  lifted); the log line of the one-time move from 4.8 / 1.9 was not captured.
- **Gunshot reverb in the big halls is weaker and much shorter.** After playing, the author found the per-room gunshot reverb "ein wenig
  too much" in the main halls of Equator and Air Pit under full-automatic fire; after hearing a first, only quieter version he named the
  long ringing as the thing to rework. The hall class covers eight rooms: Equator's atrium and its 40 m service corridor, Air Pit's main hall and passport hall, Bay5's SpawnBuilding1, Bowl's fan zone, Iceberg's cafe room and Chop Shop's main room. Its invented room table now has concrete-block walls instead of concrete, an acoustic-tile ceiling
  instead of concrete and less clutter (0.2 instead of 0.4). Measured with the developer reverb pulse on the test host, 0.5.0's table
  against the new one in the same raid, in the main halls of Equator and Air Pit: in the first part of the tail (0.35 to 0.7 s) the level is 8 to 10 dB lower at 1500 Hz and 13 to 17 dB lower at 400 Hz; from
  1.5 s after the shot it is about 32 dB lower in Air Pit at 1500 Hz and elsewhere at or near the noise floor of the measurement (at least
  36 dB lower in Equator at 1500 Hz, at least 29 and 24 dB at 400 Hz). Read from those windows, the decay time falls from about 6.5 s to
  about 2.5 s at 1500 Hz and from 9 to 14 s to about 3 to 3.5 s at 400 Hz: the old table rang longest in the low end. Bay5's spawn
  building, the loudest of the three measured halls, starts about 20 dB (1500 Hz) and 25 dB (400 Hz) lower.
  Shops and steel bodies are unchanged; at 1500 Hz a big hall now decays about as fast as a shop and is quieter, in the low end it still
  rings longer. A developer configuration that still holds an untouched earlier hall table moves once to the new one (hidden revision key
  `ShotAcousticsLab/HallDefaultsRevision`); a tuned table stays. The author listened to this table on 14 November 2026 and approved it ("passt so").

The Iceberg map, the crowd reactions and the roar layer, the neutral murmur and the Arena installation check shipped in 0.5.0 and are not
new. The contracts mode plugins bind (`SpectatorCrowd` with its eight members and `ContractVersion` 1, `ScoreMonitors`, the
`SpawnGates.GatesOpen` / `GatesOpened` done signal) are unchanged; ArenaMode 0.4.0 and later uses them. The in-game map notices are
unchanged and name no map count.

## Crowd cost

Five of the six maps have a crowd now (Iceberg has none and pays nothing): the crowds decode 135 shared images into about 0.54 GB of video
memory per raid (calculated from the image sizes: uncompressed, with mip levels; 123 images and about 0.5 GB in 0.5.0), and building a
crowd adds about two seconds to raid loading (measured on Chop Shop: 2.08 s). There is no setting for the crowds. To skip the crowd of one
map, close EFT and delete the folder `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/spectators/<map_key>` (`arena_equator`, `arena_bay5`,
`arena_airpit`, `arena_bowl` or `arena_autoservice`; Equator day and night share `arena_equator`). The next raid on that map logs a
warning that the crowd could not be restored (it can appear twice, because EFT builds the spawn points twice) and continues without the
crowd, its calls, its murmur and its reactions. Keep the `spectators/shared` folder and the other maps' folders. Extracting the release
ZIP over the installation again brings the deleted folder back.

## Requirements and compatibility

Tested with **SPT 4.1.6 / EFT 0.16.9.5-40743**; the server declares `~4.1.5`. Both client and server components are required and must come
from the same ZIP: the 0.5.0 server DLL cannot read the five-entry official map text pack of this release. An installed **Escape from
Tarkov: Arena** is required on the machines loading and registering the maps. Encrypted bundle keys target Arena
**0.6.1.0.48206** (Chop Shop's key file: 0.6.2.0.48473), and the key files of all six maps were verified on **0.6.1.0.48206**,
**0.6.2.0.48473** and the newer hotfixes **0.6.2.0.48598** and **0.6.2.0.48767**; the four maps of 0.4.0 were also verified on
**0.6.0.0.47752** (that check was not run for Iceberg or Chop Shop). An Arena update that changes the sampled map resources needs updated keys.
This requirement is not copy protection; art, audio and supporting data beside the bundles stay plaintext.

ArenaMode supplies match rules and respawns separately. DynamicMaps (tested with 1.2.1) and SAIN are optional.
Fika compatibility has not been verified. No loot or scav raids are included.

## Installation

1. Install **Escape from Tarkov: Arena** with the Battlestate Games launcher if it is not installed
   yet. It is a separate purchase from Escape from Tarkov.
2. Close EFT and the SPT server.
3. Extract the whole `maschine-ArenaMapPort-0.6.0.zip` into your SPT installation (the folder that contains `BepInEx`
   and `SPT_Runtime`), merging both folders and replacing existing ArenaMapPort files. Install both the client and
   server components.
4. Start the SPT server, then EFT.

The client folder is `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort`; its `keys` subfolder belongs to the release, so take it
from the same ZIP when you update (a missing or damaged key file is reported as an error in the client log, and the
maps do not load).
The server folder remains `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort`.

Arena is installed but not found? Set its folder in `General / ArenaInstallPath` (client,
`BepInEx/config/com.maschine.ArenaMapPort.cfg`) and in `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort/config/arena-install.json`
(server, `{"installPath": "D:\\Battlestate Games\\Escape from Tarkov Arena"}`, backslashes doubled);
both are checked like the automatic path.

**Upgrading from 0.5.0:** with EFT and the SPT server closed, extract the ZIP over the installation and keep
`BepInEx/config`. The ZIP replaces the client and the server DLL together (a running SPT server keeps its DLL locked, so close it
first) and adds Chop Shop's files: its three encrypted bundles, `keys/arena_autoservice.json`, its shader sidecar, acoustics, gate sounds,
flares, load screens, banner and crowd (`spectators/arena_autoservice`), and its server data under
`SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort/db/arena_autoservice`. It also replaces the server data (`base.json`) of the other five maps (the new
spawn rows and exits), Air Pit's changed bundles, the five crowd graphs and the shared texture manifest (add 12 shared images), the six
roar beds, the DynamicMaps files of Bay5, Air Pit, Bowl and Iceberg and the `keys` folder. **Extract everything from the ZIP:** an old
crowd graph next to the new shared texture manifest is refused by the integrity check. There are no configuration changes and no new
player settings (the four public settings are unchanged). A crowd folder you deleted under `spectators` comes back when the ZIP is
extracted; delete it again afterwards if you still want that map without its crowd.

**Upgrading from 0.4.0:** the same, and the ZIP also adds Iceberg's files, the 70 reaction clips, the six roar beds
(`arena_fans_ambient_cheer*.wav`, `arena_fans_ambient_angry*.wav`) and the neutral murmur (`arena_fans_ambient_neutral.wav`), and
replaces `spectator-audio.json`. The murmur recording of 0.4.0, `arena_fans_ambient_postmatch.wav`, stays in the folder after the update:
the client reads only the file the manifest names, so it is ignored and may be deleted.

**Upgrading from 0.3.0:** the same, and the ZIP also replaces the Equator scenes bundle with the repaired one (the file
name is the same, so it overwrites 0.3.0's) and adds the `spectators`, `gate-sounds` and `monitors` folders.
`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/parts/full.json`, the manifest of the previous ZIP, is replaced by the manifest of this ZIP.

**Upgrading from 0.2.3 or older:** the same. Every bundle has been encrypted since 0.3.0 and comes with a `keys`
folder, so the two DLLs alone are not enough. From 0.2.1 or older, the old
`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/material-shaders.json` may be deleted (Equator's shader sidecar is now
`material-shaders.arena_equator.json`).

**Upgrading a development build:** move the old `BepInEx/plugins/ArenaMapPort` directory outside
`BepInEx/plugins` before extracting, and keep your configuration files. Remove the retired
`maschine-ArenaMapDebug.dll` if present, either directly in `plugins` or inside `plugins/ArenaMapDebug`.
The README.txt in the ZIP (`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/README.txt`) covers old database/DynamicMaps
cleanup.

## Verification and known limitations

What was run, by whom and when. These are bounded checks of the builds named, not a certificate.

- **Checks on the build machine, release build, 14 November 2026:** the plugin and the server mod were built from the release tree (the
  18 bundles and the six key files came out unchanged against the development state); the .NET test suite (3,313 tests) and the Python
  suite (3,262 tests, 68 skipped) passed; the distribution validator passed for all six maps (18 bundles, all encrypted, the bundle-block
  check included); the internal names of the 18 bundles are unique; Chop Shop passed the port validator against the staged
  distribution; the key files of all six maps unlock on Arena 0.6.1.0.48206 (test host), 0.6.2.0.48473 (build machine) and
  0.6.2.0.48767 (a second PC; 0.6.2.0.48598 on that PC the day before).
- **Release build on the test host (GPU-2), 14 November 2026, 17:59 to 18:08:** server and plugin found Arena 0.6.1.0.48206 and loaded
  version 0.6.0 with six maps. One raid without bots on each map (Equator, Bay5, Air Pit, Bowl, Iceberg, Chop Shop): each map loaded
  (locations in 4.3 to 5.9 s, the five crowds in 2.08 to 2.19 s), Iceberg applied the lamp limits 4.6 / 1.8, Chop Shop put its 16 Bot
  rows on the NavMesh, and a second Chop Shop raid showed the new hall table in its main room. The hideout opened afterwards. No
  ArenaMapPort warning in the whole session. Nothing was judged by eye or ear in this run, and no bot raid was part of it.
- **Chop Shop on the test host (GPU-2), 11 to 13 November (development builds):** raids without bots and with bots (a bot walked at least 27 m in 25 seconds
  and both start areas were used as spawns), the hideout opened afterwards, 107 to 120 frames per second (120 is the cap), no ArenaMapPort warning. A held
  gate sealed the start areas for bots (bots could not reach the start areas while the gates were held, and could after the release), the exit lamps switched in
  the release frame and the gate clips played within reach. The crowd loaded in 2.08 s (30 fans, 4 groups, 70 reaction clips, 6 roar beds).
  The raid with the crowd was run on 11 November, before the spawn rows and the exits were added; those runs are the next item.
- **Spawn rows and exits on the test host, 12 and 13 November:** a plain raid on each of the six maps, no ArenaMapPort warning, every Bot
  row on the NavMesh (16, 20, 21, 17, 16 and 16 markers for Chop Shop, Air Pit, Bowl, Iceberg, Bay5 and Equator), every exit within 0.2
  m of its authored position, and two ArenaMode Team Deathmatch runs (Bowl and Iceberg) in which nobody extracted although a player stood
  in an exit box for 12 seconds or spawned inside one.
- **Sun on far walls:** checked on the test host with a live on/off control for Chop Shop (the patch is gone with the key on and back with it
  off) and for Iceberg at shadow quality Low (the lobby is lit at 08:00 and 09:00 without the rows and dark with them) and at Ultra; Medium,
  High and the evening hours were not checked.
- **Not checked for Chop Shop:** whether a player can climb the tribune decks above the hall (two small NavMesh islands were kept out by the
  bake bounds, which is inferred, not tested), the rain-depth readback, the equator shutter's collider against its detail levels seen from the
  east shop, and any ArenaMode match on this map run from this project (ArenaMode decides whether and when it offers Chop Shop).
- **Not checked for the spawn rows and exits:** a respawn that lands on an in-box marker; Team Deathmatch on Chop Shop, Air Pit and Bay5 with
  the new exits; any Domination, Deathmatch, Zombies or Tactical match with the new rows; the flares from across a map, at night or in rain,
  and the flare's sound; bots spawning at each of the 39 new rows; walking to Chop Shop's west exit (reached by teleport only). Iceberg's two exit
  boxes contain stage markers, so a first spawn can stand 1.6 m from the flare and be tinted green.
- **Listened to, Chop Shop:** the author saw Chop Shop in a first raid on 11 November ("sieht ganz gut aus") and listened to the map on
  14 November 2026: "chop shop habe ich mir angehört, passt alles". That statement covers the map as he played it; it names no single
  item (ambient mix, gate sounds, crowd or crowd reactions), so none of them is recorded as heard on its own. The ambient mix stays a
  computed start value; he asked for no change.
- **Listened to, the big-hall gunshot reverb:** the author heard its first, only quieter version on 14 November 2026 and asked for a
  shorter ring ("ich glaube es ist eher das lange nachklingen das wir nochmal überarbeiten sollten"), then listened to the final table
  the same day: "habe ich mir angehört, passt so". His approval names no room; his request named the main halls of Equator and Air Pit.
  The numbers in "Changes" are the test host's measurement, not his words: the new table was measured in Equator's and Air Pit's main
  halls and in Bay5's spawn building. The other five hall rooms got it unmeasured; Chop Shop's main room carries it by the release
  build's log line, and that room changed after the author had listened to Chop Shop earlier that day.
- **Not listened to:** Iceberg's ambient sounds, lamp loops and gate sounds; the roar beds at their new, louder level. The gate and door sounds of the other maps were checked in the log only as well.
- **Iceberg:** the 4.6 / 1.8 lamp limits (the author's values, by eye) ran in the release build's Iceberg raid on the test host. The
  one-time re-seeds of the lamp limits and of the roar-bed volume are covered by unit tests, and the test host's configuration shows both
  done; the log lines of the moves were not captured. A raid as a player from the west pocket and a bot raid
  longer than a few minutes were not run for 0.5.0 either; the raids and the ArenaMode Team Deathmatch run since then are listed above.
- **Crowd reactions and the roar layer:** unchanged since 0.5.0 (the author listened to the demo cycle on 7 November and said it sounds
  good; that is not a clip-by-clip review). **ArenaMode 0.4.0 and later has adopted the contract** and drives the reactions from its match
  events. On Chop Shop the crowd reported its 70 reaction clips in the log; no single reaction on this map is recorded as heard apart
  from the author's statement about the map as a whole.
- Gate and door sounds: Bay5's were never in range during a test, and the loudness, reach and fit of the clips shortened to the faster
  gate motion are untuned. The exit lamps were checked by reading their state, not by eye.
- Puddle footsteps: Equator's puddle cache and the rain-driven puddles have not been checked in game, and the splash sound has not been
  listened to on any map.
- A faint steady hiss and short clicks seen in one recording of Equator (before 0.4.0) are not explained by the static fix;
  the author confirmed in play on 30 October that the static itself is gone.
- EFT's own sound bundle has no impact sounds for the Books and CardBoard prop types. On Equator 39 props
  (30 Books: shoes, books, blue cups and a road cone; 9 CardBoard: pizza boxes and office folders), and any
  props of those types on other maps, stay silent when they hit something.
- Fika compatibility has not been verified. There is no loot and no scav raid on any map.
