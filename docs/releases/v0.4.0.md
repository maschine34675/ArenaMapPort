# ArenaMapPort 0.4.0

30 October 2026. Original animated crowds, spatial spectator calls, a crowd murmur, gate sounds and mode-driven
score screens for Equator, Bay5, Air Pit and Bowl.

## Changes since 0.3.0

- Original animated spectators on all four maps: 99 on Air Pit, Equator day/night and Bowl, 88 on Bay5.
  Fifteen appearances and original idle/cheering animations run at staggered intervals. The missing legacy
  face cloth uses Arena's current half-mask. The spectators are decoration and do not affect gameplay.
  They cost video memory and loading time, see "Crowd cost" below.
- Thirteen original fan recordings play from nearby spectators through native spatial audio and room
  obstruction. One call starts every 4 to 8 seconds, the first one 3 to 8 seconds after the raid starts.
  The longest recording lasts 3.3 seconds, so calls no longer overlap; the limit of two voices at once stays
  as a guard. Speakers and voice families do not duplicate. Scav phrases are excluded; kill and
  round-result reactions are not implemented.
- A quiet, continuous crowd murmur plays from every group of spectators on all four maps. The spectators of
  a map are sorted into two to six groups by where they stand, and each group has its own looping source at
  its centre, with a different start point and a slightly different pitch so the groups do not repeat in step.
  The murmur is spatial and fades with distance. It is Arena's own crowd-ambience recording, the only one in
  its files: Arena names it as a post-match sound and its maps carry no crowd ambience, so the murmur is an
  addition of this port, not something these maps play in Arena. The recording is mixed to mono and its loop
  seam is smoothed. The murmur is a deliberate background of voices; it is not the static or the faint hiss
  described under Known limitations. It stays at one level: it does not react to match events yet. A mode plugin
  can raise it by up to 60 percent through the public class `ArenaMapPort.Client.SpectatorCrowd` (contract
  version 1: `SetExcitement`, `Excitement`, `IsAvailable`); ArenaMode does not use it yet. The audio file is
  about 2.6 MB. There is no player setting; the developer settings `Spectators/CrowdBed`,
  `CrowdBedVolume` and `CrowdBedReach` need `General/DeveloperMode`.
- The spectator textures are stored once and shared by all four maps (about 147 MB on disk). Integrity
  checks detect missing, damaged or mismatched map/texture packages.
- Gate and door sounds are added on all four maps: when a mode plugin releases held spawn gates, Arena's
  own sounds start at them. This was confirmed in the log on Equator, Air Pit and Bowl but not yet by ear,
  see Known limitations. Exit lamps on Equator and Air Pit switch with those gates (checked by reading
  their state, not by eye).
- Original TV screens can show a mode's timer, team scores and alive counts through the optional
  ScoreMonitors integration. They remain unchanged without a mode plugin using the interface.
- Puddle footsteps are added on Equator, Air Pit and Bowl, in addition to Bay5. The Air Pit and Bowl
  caches were checked in the log; Equator's cache and the rain-driven puddles have not yet been checked in
  game, and the splash was not listened to.
- Four Equator/Bay5 spawn positions moved slightly inboard from the navigation edge.
- A redundant map-error dialog is suppressed after EFT already explained the same failed load.
- Late collisions from remaining Arena props after a map is unloaded no longer request the already
  unloaded native sound bundle.
- A cause of the intermittent short static on Equator was found and fixed: one block of the Equator scenes bundle
  was played as noise in place of part of the indoor wind sound. What has and has not been checked is under Known
  limitations.

## Crowd cost

The crowds decode 123 shared images into about 0.5 GB of video memory per raid on every map
(calculated from the image sizes: uncompressed, with mip levels), and building a crowd adds about
two seconds to raid loading. There is no setting for the crowds. To skip the crowd of
one map, close EFT and delete the folder `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/spectators/<map_key>`
(`arena_equator`, `arena_bay5`, `arena_airpit` or `arena_bowl`; Equator day and night share `arena_equator`).
The next raid on that map logs a warning that the crowd could not be restored (it can appear twice, because
EFT builds the spawn points twice) and continues as in 0.3.0, without the crowd, its calls and its murmur. Keep the
`spectators/shared` folder and the other maps' folders. Extracting the release ZIP over the installation again brings the deleted folder back.

## Requirements and compatibility

Tested with **SPT 4.1.6 / EFT 0.16.9.5-40743**; the server declares `~4.1.5`. Both client and server
components are required. An installed **Escape from Tarkov: Arena** is required on the machines
loading/registering the maps. Encrypted bundle keys target Arena **0.6.1.0.48206** and were also verified
with **0.6.0.0.47752**. An Arena update that changes the sampled map resources needs updated keys.
This requirement is not copy protection; art, audio and supporting data beside the bundles stay plaintext.

ArenaMode supplies match rules and respawns separately. DynamicMaps (tested with 1.2.1) and SAIN are optional.
Fika compatibility has not been verified. No loot or scav raids are included.

## Installation

1. Install **Escape from Tarkov: Arena** with the Battlestate Games launcher if it is not installed
   yet. It is a separate purchase from Escape from Tarkov.
2. Close EFT and the SPT server.
3. Extract the whole `maschine-ArenaMapPort-0.4.0.zip` into your SPT installation (the folder that contains `BepInEx`
   and `SPT_Runtime`), merging both folders and replacing existing ArenaMapPort files. Install both the client and
   server components.
4. Start the SPT server, then EFT.

The client folder is `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort`; its `keys` subfolder belongs to the release, so take it
from the same ZIP when you update (a missing or damaged key file is reported as an error in the client log, and the
maps do not load).
The server folder remains `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort`.

Arena is installed but not found? Set its folder in `General / ArenaInstallPath` (client,
`BepInEx/config/com.maschine.ArenaMapPort.cfg`) and in `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort/config/arena-install.json`
(server, `{"installPath": "D:\\Battlestate Games\\Escape from Tarkov Arena"}`, backslashes doubled);
both are checked like the automatic path.

**Upgrading from 0.3.0:** with EFT and the SPT server closed, extract the ZIP over the installation and keep
`BepInEx/config`. The ZIP replaces the client and the server DLL together (a running SPT server keeps its DLL
locked, so close it first), replaces the Equator scenes bundle with the repaired one (the file name is the same, so
it overwrites 0.3.0's) and adds the `spectators` and `spectator-audio` folders (crowds, calls and murmur), the
`gate-sounds` folder and the `monitors` folder. `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/parts/full.json`, the manifest of 0.3.0's
ZIP, is replaced by the manifest of this ZIP. 0.3.0 needs no spectator cleanup because that release contained none.

**Upgrading from 0.2.3 or older:** the same. Every bundle has been encrypted since 0.3.0 and comes with a `keys`
folder, so the two DLLs alone are not enough. From 0.2.1 or older, the old
`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/material-shaders.json` may be deleted (Equator's shader sidecar is now
`material-shaders.arena_equator.json`).

**Upgrading a development build:** move the old `BepInEx/plugins/ArenaMapPort` directory outside
`BepInEx/plugins` before extracting, and keep your configuration files. Remove the retired
`maschine-ArenaMapDebug.dll` if present, either directly in `plugins` or inside `plugins/ArenaMapDebug`.
The README.txt in the ZIP (`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/README.txt`) covers old database/DynamicMaps
cleanup. Installations with earlier per-map spectator PNGs can retain those unused copies safely, but they consume about 440 MB of extra space. After
installing and verifying this release, remove only the `.png` files directly inside
`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/spectators/arena_airpit`, `arena_equator`, `arena_bay5` and
`arena_bowl`. Keep each `spectator.json.gz` and the complete `spectators/shared` folder. The development
deployment script provides `-RemoveLegacySpectatorTextures` (dry run first, then `-Confirm`); it refuses
unknown/changed files or incomplete replacements.

## Verification and known limitations

The [29 October native sweep](https://github.com/maschine34675/ArenaMapPort/blob/v0.4.0/docs/progress/release-sweep-20261029.md) exercised all four maps on GPU-2,
including Equator day/night, animated crowds, bot combat/objectives, native match ends and resource cleanup.
The final Bowl rerun passed after the late physics-sound fix. The source suite passed 2,545 tests and
the client Release build had zero warnings/errors. These are bounded checks of the swept client;
they do not certify subjective audio quality or the hashes of a later release rebuild. The crowd murmur and
the longer pauses between spectator calls came after the sweep and are not part of it.

- Gate and door sounds were checked in the log only and have not been listened to. Bay5's gate sounds were
  never in range during a test, and their loudness, reach and the fit of the clips shortened to the
  faster gate motion are untuned.
- Puddle footsteps: Equator's puddle cache and the rain-driven puddles have not been checked in game, and
  the splash sound has not been listened to on any map.
- The crowd murmur and the 4-to-8-second pauses between spectator calls are new. They ran on the test host on all four
  maps without a warning, and the author listened to them in play on 30 October 2026 and approved them; their level and
  reach are a first choice, tunable with DeveloperMode.
- A cause of the intermittent short static on Equator was found and fixed in this release. It was a defect in the
  mod's own Equator bundle, not the game's mix and not an extra sound: one 128 KiB block of the Equator scenes
  bundle carried a compression flag although it was stored at full size (a boundary case in a library the mod's
  build uses), so the game played the block's raw bytes as about four seconds of static in the indoor wind clip,
  once per wind cycle. The bundle is repaired, every bundle is now checked for such blocks before it is staged,
  and the static no longer shows up in recordings of the game's sound made on the test host. 0.3.0's Equator
  bundle carries the defect; the Equator scenes bundle of this release replaces it.
- The author confirmed in play on 30 October 2026 that the Equator static is gone. A faint steady hiss and short
  clicks seen in one recording are not explained by the fix.
- EFT's own sound bundle has no impact sounds for the Books and CardBoard prop types. On Equator 39 props
  (30 Books: shoes, books, blue cups and a road cone; 9 CardBoard: pizza boxes and office folders), and any
  props of those types on other maps, stay silent when they hit something.
