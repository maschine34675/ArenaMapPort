# ArenaMapPort 0.3.0

Brings **Bowl**, an open-air stadium arena, from Escape from Tarkov: Arena to SPT, next to **Air Pit**,
**Equator (Day/Night)** and **Bay5**. The scenes and navigation of those three maps are unchanged from 0.2.2.

## Included

- **Bowl:** the stadium arena from Arena. The sunken stadium field with a crashed Mi-26 in the middle, containers and
  guard towers, and a gated team pocket at each end (east and west). Like Bay5 and Air Pit it has two normal
  server-time choices, a progressing day/night cycle and dynamic weather. Bot spawns, waves and PMC squads are
  included, and two gates (Bowl BTR Gate and Bowl Army Gate) serve as extractions. The spectator stands are scenery.
- **Air Pit:** an abandoned terminal hangar from Arena. It contains the furnished main hall
  with its four shops, the passport hall and the apron. Like Bay5 it has two normal server-time
  choices, a progressing day/night cycle and dynamic weather. Bot spawns, waves and PMC squads
  are included, and two gates (Air Pit North Gate and Air Pit South Gate) serve as extractions.
- **Equator** with a realistic daytime appearance and an optional neon midnight appearance.
- **Bay5** with normal time progression and dynamic weather.
- Imported geometry, materials, lighting, audio, collision and navigation, plus
  map-specific repairs.
- Optional DynamicMaps overview maps for all four maps, stored inside ArenaMapPort's own
  directory. Air Pit and Bowl show their ground floor; their upper spectator areas are omitted.
- Original map descriptions in English, German and Russian, with English fallback.
- Per-room gunshot reverb on all four maps: shots ring differently in a big hall than in a
  small shop. The room data is invented, because neither EFT nor Arena provides gunshot
  reverb values; outdoors the shipped acoustics stay. It can be switched off per map under
  `Audio (<map key>)` / `RoomShotAcoustics` with `General/DeveloperMode` enabled (enable it
  and restart EFT).
- Spawn gates for mode plugins (introduced in 0.2.2).

The maps are intended for **ArenaMode**, which is installed separately. No loot or scav raids
are provided. Tested with **SPT 4.1.6 / EFT 0.16.9.5-40743**; server compatibility is `~4.1.5`.
Optional DynamicMaps integration was tested with 1.2.1.

## Changes since 0.2.2

- **New map: Bowl.** What it carries over from the other maps and what is specific to it:
  - Arena's own content on top of the geometry: projection decals, ballistic surfaces, light culling and
    flicker, small physics props that react when touched, ambient audio and room acoustics. The per-room
    gunshot reverb of the other maps is on here too. A fresh navigation mesh covers the field and both team
    pockets as one connected area.
  - Player spawns sit in the two pockets. Bots spawn on Arena's AI core points and patrol the field along a patrol
    way built from Arena's own patrol points. Each extraction box lies just outside one pocket's gate.
  - The two pockets sit behind gates (the east pocket's gate and BTR, the west pocket's army gate and
    ambulance). In an ordinary raid they open at raid start. They are registered with the spawn gates, so a mode
    plugin can hold them closed until its fight starts (see 0.2.2); the hold seals both pockets for bots and players
    and the release opens them again (verified with the developer test hold).
  - The play area is bounded by Arena's invisible border walls, which Bowl keeps because the stadium has no
    other bound. The navigation mesh ends at them.
  - The tunnel tarp uses a stand-in shader: Arena's reflective two-sided shader does not exist in EFT, so the
    tarp shows its cloth texture on both sides without the reflection.
  - The DynamicMaps overview covers the ground floor: the field, both pockets (drawn open), the outline of the
    Mi-26 and the containers. The stands, the Mi-26's roof and the tower platforms are not drawn.
  - Bowl has its own banner, four loading screens (Arena ships no loading screen for Bowl, so these are its four
    main-menu backdrops), its original description in three languages and its own position on the region-selection
    artwork.
- **Gunshot reverb in steel bodies.** A large thick-steel room, such as Bowl's crashed Mi-26, now gets the metal
  room class (steel walls) instead of the plasterboard room. The rooms of the other three maps keep their classes.
- **Weather after map changes.** A ported map's cached weather rig could lose its sky-provider reference after a
  vanilla raid in the same session, which logged repeated native weather, distant-shadow and ambient-highlight
  exceptions on the next ported raid (seen on Equator after Air Pit, Customs and Labyrinth in one session). The
  reference is restored now.
- **Distribution.** The ZIP carries four maps. Air Pit's, Equator's and Bay5's bundles are unchanged, but
  Bowl's bundles are new, so upgrading needs the full ZIP (no DLL-only package).

## Known limitations

- **No grass on Bowl.** Arena's grass for Bowl is a GPU-instanced grass scene, which is not ported.
- **Tree wind is not ported.** The ported scenes carry no tree wind settings, so EFT's wind calculation for
  trees is never set up and Bowl's trees are not animated by it. EFT logs `Invalid windSettings` once
  while a ported map loads; this is harmless, it comes from the same missing settings, and vanilla maps do not
  log it.
- **The stands are scenery.** The spectator stands above the field are empty and not part of the play area.
- **The capture point lies under the Mi-26.** Arena's capture point for this mode sits under the crashed
  helicopter's belly, where there is no walkable ground; where objectives go on Bowl is up to the mode plugin.
- **No lamp flares on Bowl.** Equator and Bay5 draw Arena's original lamp flares. Bowl's only active flare lights
  belong to the distant city backdrop, farther away than the 500 m the flare renderer draws, so none appear.

## Installation

1. Close EFT and the SPT server.
2. Extract `maschine-ArenaMapPort-0.3.0.zip` into your SPT installation, merging `BepInEx`
   and `SPT_Runtime`. Install both the client and server components.
3. Start the SPT server, then EFT.

The client folder is `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort`.
The server folder remains `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort`.

**Upgrading from 0.2.2:** with EFT and the SPT server closed, extract the ZIP over the
installation (Bowl's bundles are new, so the two DLLs alone are not enough). Keep `BepInEx/config`.

**Upgrading from 0.2.1 or 0.2.0:** the same, and the old
`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/material-shaders.json` may be deleted.

**Upgrading from 0.1.0:** with EFT and the SPT server closed, extract the ZIP over the
installation. Keep `BepInEx/config`; the configuration migrations run automatically.

**Upgrading a development build:** move the old `BepInEx/plugins/ArenaMapPort` directory
outside `BepInEx/plugins` before extracting. Keep your `BepInEx/config` settings.
Remove the retired `maschine-ArenaMapDebug.dll` if present, either directly in `plugins`
or inside `plugins/ArenaMapDebug`. The included README covers old database/DynamicMaps cleanup.

F12 exposes `NaturalEquatorNight` to use the natural appearance in the next Equator night raid.
`DeveloperMode` is an advanced setting and is off by default.
