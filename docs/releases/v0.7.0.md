# ArenaMapPort 0.7.0

<release-date>. Block, the seventh Arena map, joins Equator, Bay5, Air Pit, Bowl, Iceberg and Chop Shop. Arena's own grass now grows on Bowl,
Iceberg and Block, drops run down the screen in rain on every map, and Chop Shop and Block have DynamicMaps overviews, with support for DynamicMaps 1.3.0.

## Changes since 0.6.0

- **Block (new map).** The courtyard of a residential city block from Escape from Tarkov: Arena (Arena's `Arena_Yard`, called Block in
  Arena's own texts) as a seventh location, "Arena: Block": apartment houses around a yard with a fortified playground tower, parked vans and
  wrecked cars. It uses Arena's TeamFight layout on a play area of about 87 x 174 m: two team pockets about 130 m apart, each sealed
  until its gate opens, one capture point and two moving gates, a rising facade gondola at the south pocket and two swinging leaves at
  the north pocket. In a plain raid both gates stand open; a mode plugin can hold and release them. The map has Arena's own scenes,
  materials and colliders, a newly baked NavMesh for bots, Arena's room acoustics (19 rooms) and ambient sounds (50 players, 145 clips),
  104 invisible border walls that keep the play area closed, and two exits ("Block South Gate" and "Block North Gate", 8 seconds), one in
  each team pocket. Time of day and weather follow the server, as on Bay5. No loot and no scav raids, as on every map. What Block has:
  - Arena's own spectator crowd: 27 fans on the balconies, with the calls, the murmur and the reactions of the other crowd maps (four
    groups of spectators for the murmur and the roar beds).
  - Arena's gate and door sounds (three rows, two clips, played when a mode plugin releases held gates), Arena's door and gate end
    positions (with an overshoot at the end of the gondola's rise, as in Arena) and eight TV screens, all with a face, for the ScoreMonitors
    contract (ArenaMode's score display). Like the other maps, these do nothing in an ordinary raid; a mode plugin claims the screens through
    the ScoreMonitors contract (unchanged).
  - 4 Player rows and 18 Bot rows (original rows, five spawn waves and four PMC squads), loading screens (five), a menu banner, an official
    description in Arena's own words and a selection marker on EFT's region map, shown at a spot that does not cover another region's label
    (read back on the test host's selection screen).
  - What it does not have: **no per-light flares** (none of its lights is active in Arena's scenes), **no native
    water**, **no winter or snow look** (summer set only) and **no ArenaMode entry from this project**: Block is registered as a location in SPT;
    whether and when ArenaMode offers it is ArenaMode's decision, and this project has run no ArenaMode match on it.
  - The author looked at and listened to the map on 21 November 2026 and approved it as a whole: "sieht gut aus, auch nachts, ist dann halt wie
    die anderen maps im außenbereich recht dunkel. hört sich gut an, ambient sounds können etwas lauter sein (gain auf 5, range auf 3)". The
    sentence names no single item (see "Verification and known limitations"). Its ambient sound mix is the author's own, chosen by ear:
    5 dB, offset 0.1, range 3 (the computed start value was 3 dB, offset 0.1, range 2). A saved, untouched start mix moves once to it; any
    other saved mix stays (hidden revision key; every other map only advances its stamp). On 22 November the author looked at the game
    again and wrote "Block passt, Regentropfen auch, mir fiel aber gerade auf dass das Gras sich nicht im Wind bewegt, bekommen wir das auch
    noch hin? beim drüberlaufen wird es zumindest platt gedrückt": Block as a whole again, no single item named (not the marker or the
    overview), and the rain drops and the grass, below.
- **Arena's own grass on Bowl, Iceberg and Block, on by default.** Tufts with seed stalks along walls, kerbs and lawns: Bowl (113,643
  clumps), Iceberg (15,410, standing on its hillside) and Block (10,132, on the lawns of the courtyard). Equator, Bay5, Air Pit and Chop Shop
  have no grass in Arena. The grass comes from a fourth encrypted bundle per map (`arena_bowl_grass.bundle`, `arena_iceberg_grass.bundle` and `arena_yard_grass.bundle`, 18.9, 12.2 and 11.9 MB) that opens
  with the same key as the map's other bundles, so the installed Arena is still required. It has no collision and no footstep or bullet effect; it is pressed flat when walked over (the game's own
  trampling) and it sways in the wind. The first version stood still in the wind, because the game's grass shader has wind code but a
  ported map has no wind calculator to advance its phase; the port now advances it the way the game's calculator would, and strength and
  direction follow the weather's wind. There is no player setting. Measured on the test host at 1920 x 1080 with the game's frame limiter lifted (one
  sample per view): about 0.13 to 0.27 ms per frame (Bowl about 0.16 to 0.24 ms, Iceberg about 0.27 ms, Block about 0.13 ms; measured before the sway was added, not repeated with the limiter lifted); at the limiter's 120
  frames per second the figures stayed at the baseline, and Block read 119.5 with the sway. **The author saw the grass standing still and asked for the sway; after the sway was added the author wrote "ja das Gras passt jetzt" (22 November 2026); the sentence names no map and no single aspect.** Earlier notes and the README said that no grass is
  ported on Bowl and Iceberg: that no longer holds. Extract the whole ZIP: replacing only the DLLs gives a "Required map file is missing"
  warning and no grass.
- **Rain drops on the screen, on every map, on by default.** In rain, in the open and not under a roof, drops now run down the screen. Until now
  the game's "camera under rain" flag was false for ever on a ported map, so no drop ever appeared. The repair answers that flag from the same
  depth photo that the falling rain already uses, so drops and streaks agree: no drops under a roof or in a hall, drops under Equator's open
  lattice. Drops stop when you look steeply at the ground. The same game flag also drives the water on glasses, the ripples on wet ground
  and the rain on wet props; **nobody has looked at those**. It changes the six older maps in rain outdoors as well as Block. There is no
  player setting. **The author has seen the drops and found them fine** ("Block passt, Regentropfen auch", 22 November 2026; the sentence
  names no map).
- **Chop Shop has a DynamicMaps overview.** One ground view of the hall, the yard, both team pockets and the raised north route, with both
  exit markers: two files in the plugin's own `dynamicmaps/ArenaMapPort_AutoService` folder, nothing to copy into DynamicMaps. Open the
  inventory and then MAP during a raid. Its original in-game checks used DynamicMaps 1.2.1.
- **Block has a DynamicMaps overview.** One north-up plan combines both team pockets and the connected raised routes, with both exit
  markers. Its two files live in `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/dynamicmaps/ArenaMapPort_Yard`; DynamicMaps' own folder stays
  untouched. The western dead-end alley outside the bot navigation bake is shown as muted physical context.
- **DynamicMaps 1.3.0 support.** The loader adapts to the 1.3.0 and 1.2.1 APIs, adds owned maps before preference registration and retains
  unchanged map instances when the catalogue is scanned again. On the test host with 1.3.0 the loader and Block's overview were run in game and all seven overviews were opened once on an earlier build than the release build; the retained 1.2.1 adapter was checked
  offline for this update. See the exact native build and scope under "Verification".
- The ZIP grows to about <zip-gib> GiB (<file-count> files; 0.6.0: about 5.9 GiB, 396 files): Block's 24 files (its grass bundle and overview included), the
  grass bundles of Bowl and Iceberg and Chop Shop's two overview files.

The Chop Shop map, the spawn rows and exits, the hall reverb and the contracts mode plugins bind (`SpectatorCrowd` with its eight members and
`ContractVersion` 1, `ScoreMonitors`, the `SpawnGates.GatesOpen` / `GatesOpened` done signal) are unchanged; ArenaMode 0.4.0 and later
uses them. The in-game map notices are unchanged and name no map count. The four public settings are unchanged.

## Crowd cost

Six of the seven maps have a crowd (Iceberg has none and pays nothing). Block's 27 fans use the images that the other crowds already share:
the 135 shared images (about 0.54 GB of video memory per raid, calculated from the image sizes: uncompressed, with mip levels) do not change,
and building a crowd adds about two seconds to raid loading (measured on Block: 2.19 s). There is no setting for the crowds. To skip the crowd
of one map, close EFT and delete the folder `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/spectators/<map_key>` (`arena_equator`, `arena_bay5`,
`arena_airpit`, `arena_bowl`, `arena_autoservice` or `arena_yard`; Equator day and night share `arena_equator`). The next raid on that map logs a
warning that the crowd could not be restored (it can appear twice, because EFT builds the spawn points twice) and continues without the
crowd, its calls, its murmur and its reactions. Keep the `spectators/shared` folder and the other maps' folders. Extracting the release
ZIP over the installation again brings the deleted folder back.

## Requirements and compatibility

Tested with **SPT 4.1.6 / EFT 0.16.9.5-40743**; the server declares `~4.1.5`. Both client and server components are required and must come
from the same ZIP: the 0.6.0 server DLL does not know Block. An
installed **Escape from Tarkov: Arena** is required on the machines loading and registering the maps. Encrypted bundle keys target Arena
**0.6.1.0.48206** (the key files of Chop Shop and Block: 0.6.2.0.48473). Block's key file was verified on **0.6.1.0.48206**, **0.6.2.0.48473** and **0.6.2.0.48767**
(not on 0.6.2.0.48598, nor on 0.6.0.0.47752); the key files of the six older maps were verified for
0.6.0 on **0.6.1.0.48206**, **0.6.2.0.48473**, **0.6.2.0.48598** and **0.6.2.0.48767**, and the four maps of 0.4.0 also on **0.6.0.0.47752**
(that check was not run for Iceberg, Chop Shop or Block). An Arena update that changes the sampled map resources needs updated keys. This
requirement is not copy protection; art, audio and supporting data beside the bundles stay plaintext.

ArenaMode supplies match rules and respawns separately. DynamicMaps and SAIN are optional. All seven overview packs remain in
ArenaMapPort's own mod folder. On the test host with DynamicMaps 1.3.0 the loader (Iceberg and Factory) and Block's overview were run in game, and all seven overviews were opened once on an earlier build than the release build; the retained 1.2.1 adapter was
checked offline against its DLL for this update and has not been run in game since the loader changed (its last in-game runs predate the change). The release build's own checks are under Verification. Fika compatibility has not been verified. No loot or scav raids are included.

## Installation

1. Install **Escape from Tarkov: Arena** with the Battlestate Games launcher if it is not installed
   yet. It is a separate purchase from Escape from Tarkov.
2. Close EFT and the SPT server.
3. Extract the whole `maschine-ArenaMapPort-0.7.0.zip` into your SPT installation (the folder that contains `BepInEx`
   and `SPT_Runtime`), merging both folders and replacing existing ArenaMapPort files. Install both the client and
   server components.
4. Start the SPT server, then EFT.

The client folder is `BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort`; its `keys` subfolder belongs to the release, so take it
from the same ZIP when you update (a missing or damaged key file is reported as an error in the client log, and the
maps do not load).
The server folder remains `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort`.

Arena is installed but not found? Set its folder in `General / ArenaInstallPath` (client,
`BepInEx/config/com.maschine.ArenaMapPort.cfg`) and in `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort/config/arena-install.json`
(server, `{"installPath": "D:\\Battlestate Games\\Escape from Tarkov Arena"}`, backslashes doubled);
both are checked like the automatic path.

**Upgrading from 0.6.0:** with EFT and the SPT server closed, extract the ZIP over the installation and keep
`BepInEx/config`. Nothing has to be deleted: by the plan's counts the ZIP only adds files (396 files in 0.6.0, <file-count> now). It replaces
the client and the server DLL together (a running SPT server keeps its DLL locked, so close it first) and adds Block's files: its
four encrypted bundles (scenes, NavMesh, preset and grass), `keys/arena_yard.json`, its shader sidecar, acoustics, gate sounds, load
screens, banner, AI file and crowd (`spectators/arena_yard`), its overview under `dynamicmaps/ArenaMapPort_Yard`, and its server data under
`SPT_Runtime/user/mods/ArenaMapPort/db/arena_yard`.
It also adds the grass bundles of Bowl and Iceberg and Chop Shop's two overview files, and replaces the official map text pack and the
`parts/full.json` manifest. **Extract everything from the ZIP:** the grass bundles and the DLLs belong together (with the new DLL but no grass
bundle the client logs a missing-file warning and the map has no grass). There are no manual configuration changes and no new player settings (the
four public settings are unchanged). A crowd folder you deleted under `spectators` comes back when the ZIP is extracted; delete it again
afterwards if you still want that map without its crowd.

**Upgrading from 0.5.0:** the same, and the ZIP also adds Chop Shop's files, replaces the server data (`base.json`) of the older maps (the
new spawn rows and exits), Air Pit's changed bundles, the crowd graphs and the shared texture manifest (add 12 shared images), the six roar
beds, the DynamicMaps files of Bay5, Air Pit, Bowl and Iceberg and the `keys` folder. An old crowd graph next to the new shared texture
manifest is refused by the integrity check.

**Upgrading from 0.4.0:** the same, and the ZIP also adds Iceberg's files, the 70 reaction clips, the six roar beds
(`arena_fans_ambient_cheer*.wav`, `arena_fans_ambient_angry*.wav`) and the neutral murmur (`arena_fans_ambient_neutral.wav`), and
replaces `spectator-audio.json`. The murmur recording of 0.4.0, `arena_fans_ambient_postmatch.wav`, stays in the folder after the update:
the client reads only the file the manifest names, so it is ignored and may be deleted.

**Upgrading from 0.3.0:** the same, and the ZIP also replaces the Equator scenes bundle with the repaired one (the file
name is the same, so it overwrites 0.3.0's) and adds the `spectators`, `gate-sounds` and `monitors` folders.
`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/parts/full.json`, the manifest of the previous ZIP, is replaced by the manifest of this ZIP.

**Upgrading from 0.2.3 or older:** the same. Every bundle has been encrypted since 0.3.0 and comes with a `keys`
folder, so the two DLLs alone are not enough. From 0.2.1 or older, the old
`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/material-shaders.json` may be deleted (Equator's shader sidecar is now
`material-shaders.arena_equator.json`).

**Upgrading a development build:** move the old `BepInEx/plugins/ArenaMapPort` directory outside
`BepInEx/plugins` before extracting, and keep your configuration files. Remove the retired
`maschine-ArenaMapDebug.dll` if present, either directly in `plugins` or inside `plugins/ArenaMapDebug`.
The README.txt in the ZIP (`BepInEx/plugins/maschine-ArenaMapPort/README.txt`) covers old database/DynamicMaps
cleanup.
