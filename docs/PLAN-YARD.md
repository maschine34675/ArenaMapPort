modes and navigation, S3 environment, rendering and audio, S4 pipeline gap against the Autoservice precedent, S5 grass). The probes

Status lives in [HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md) and the progress log [docs/progress/yard-release.md](progress/yard-release.md) (survey
record, dated work, host evidence). This file is the plan, not the record. Work happens on the branch `feature/yard` (created

Yard is Arena's `Arena_Yard` (player-facing name "Block" in Arena's own locale: the key `Arena_Yard` and the Name row of Arena's location
id `64b7d7f23d81c8a9ee03ac27` hold the same short word in en and ge; the port's `id_field` `187d5cbc21744c97e8348e76` has no row in any
locale table). Preset `arena_yard_preset.bundle` (ServerName `Arena_Yard`, no MongoId field), port key `arena_yard`, source mode TeamFight.
It is a residential courtyard (apartment block, playground tower, vans, a burnt car, a stairwell breach; seen in the banner and loading
art): the play area is the largest of the released candidates, a main NavMesh component of 87 x 174 m (8,064.5 m2, 4.2 times Chop Shop's
1,916 m2, 1.7 times Iceberg's 4,788 m2) on a floor at y 6.15 (92.5 % of cells), decks to y 10.7, buildings to y 30; about 74 % of the
walkable cells are open to the sky (inferred, vertical rays). TeamFight has two tiny SEALED team pockets 130 m apart in z (south 91 m2
behind a rising gondola, north 126 m2 behind two rotating gate leaves), 20 `SpawnPointMarker`s, two `SPAWN_BOUND_FIGHTER` boxes, one capture point and
three animated gate parts. Yard is the first map whose grass data is its own file and can be priced (10,132 clumps; Bowl and Iceberg also have Grass scenes) and the first with two room-tone clips. The preset also offers
ShootOutSolo/Duo/Trio, Coop, LastHero, LastHero_winter and BlastGang (TDS); no CheckPoint or Domination preset (an orphan Domination-like
scene 228 exists). It has 27 `ArenaFanEntity` spectators (full-body rigs like Chop Shop's, under a disabled root like Air Pit's), so it is not crowdless.
